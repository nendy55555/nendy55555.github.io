— About Bio / Issue 01

I sit at the intersection of strategy and execution.

In Deloitte's Tech, Data & AI Strategy practice, I help Fortune 500 clients turn ambitious technology bets into programs that actually ship. The edge isn't the frameworks — it's that I write code. Prototypes, internal tools, weekend experiments.

This site is the index of those experiments. Each one started as a problem I ran into myself and grew into something I could point at. Strategy is more honest when the strategist has shipped.
